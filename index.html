The situation

It says delivered.
But where is it?

A buyer's tracking shows their order was delivered, but their package hasn't arrived.

My approach

  1. Acknowledge the concern.
  2. Check the tracking details.
  3. Suggest practical checks.
  4. Give clear next steps and timelines.
Empathy + clear next steps
@Sample email replyCustomer care
SubjectUpdate on your order #123456789

Hi Maria,

Thank you for letting us know, and I'm sorry for the inconvenience this has caused. I understand how frustrating it is when tracking says delivered but the package isn't with you.

I've checked your tracking details, and it shows the package was marked delivered on October 3. To help us resolve this quickly, could you please:

  1. Check around your home, mailbox, and with neighbors or building reception.
  2. Reply with any details that might help, such as a photo of the delivery area.

If the package still can't be found by October 8, we'll move forward with a replacement or refund, whichever you prefer. I'll keep following up until this is resolved.

Warm regards,
Argine

The situation

A specialist review.
A customer kept informed.

A buyer's concern needs to be passed to another team, and they want to know what happens next.

My approach

  1. Reassure the buyer.
  2. Explain the escalation.
  3. Set expectations on timing.
  4. Keep the conversation open.
Reassurance + follow-through

Hi Daniel,

Thank you for your patience. I've escalated your case to our specialist team for a closer review, so you won't need to explain the issue again.

Thank you for your understanding. I'm here if you need anything in the meantime.

Fast replies. Still personal.

Six sample macros for common customer concerns. Open a topic to read the reply.

01Order status

Hi Adelle, thanks for reaching out! Your order #123456 is currently in transit and is expected to arrive by October 10. You can track it here: track.example.com/123456. Let me know if you need anything else.

02Refund request received

Hi Mark, I'm sorry about the trouble with your order #2468. I've started your refund request, and it should be processed within 24 to 48 hours. I'll update you as soon as it's confirmed.

03Handling an upset customer

Hi Ben, I completely understand your frustration, and I'm sorry for the trouble. Let me look into this right now and get you a clear answer as soon as possible.

04Asking for more details

Hi Pete, to look into this for you, could you please send a photo, your order number, or a description? Once I have it, I'll get back to you right away.

05Closing the case

Hi Sam, I'm glad we could get this sorted. If anything else comes up, just reply to this message and I'll be happy to help.

06Wrong item received

Hi Carlo, I'm sorry you received the wrong item. Could you please send a photo of the item and the packaging? Once I have it, I'll arrange a replacement or refund, whichever you prefer.
